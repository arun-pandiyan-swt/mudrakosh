1. PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING & USER WORKFLOW (Word Count: ~390 words)
======================================================================

The Directorate of Printing & Stationery (DPS), Government of Assam, operates a central inventory and manufacturing ecosystem from its Bamunimaidam Central Depot and Government Press in Guwahati. It procures, stores, converts, and distributes thousands of metric tonnes of paper (Maplitho, Azure Laid, Kraft, Bond, Art Paper), printing consumables, statutory judicial forms, budget volumes, electoral registers, and weekly Assam Gazettes across all state departments, district commissionerates, courts, and legislative bodies.

Who is affected & primary beneficiaries:
1. Directorate Leadership & Sanction Authorities: Must balance statewide quarterly quotas, prevent stockouts of critical stationery, and answer legislative audits.
2. Central Storekeepers & Officers: Manage intake of bulk mill consignments, storehouse rack allocations, and outward dispatches.
3. Assam Government Press Personnel: Issue raw paper reels to high-speed web/offset presses and must reconcile machine output against spoilage.
4. Recipient Department DDOs & Nodal Officers: Over 100+ state offices that indent stationery and historically wait weeks for physical dispatches.
5. Delivery Drivers & Gate Security: Coordinate physical transport and gate entry/exit verification.

The current workflow & operational difficulties:
- Manual, Fragmented Paper Registers: Material movements are recorded across physical ledgers, bin cards, and peon delivery dispatch books. Tallying balances across multiple store bays requires days of physical stocktaking.
- Delayed Stockout Identification: Reorder thresholds are managed manually. Critical items (e.g., Judicial Stamp Paper, Gazette reels) frequently breach safety thresholds before procurement requisitions are initiated.
- The "Press Floor Black Hole": Paper issued to press lines is recorded as consumed on issuance, without real-time tracking of printed impressions, unprinted Return-to-Store (RTS) reels, or machine trimming spoilage against the statutory 3.5% CAG ceiling.
- Transit Blind Spots & Lost Proof-of-Delivery: Outward consignments travel via truck with physical delivery challans. Discrepancies between dispatched quantities and received quantities often emerge months later during audit reconciliations.

Specific gaps addressed:
1. Absence of a real-time, tamper-evident central inventory ledger with bay/rack level granularity.
2. Disconnected paper conversion tracking between central stores and press machines.
3. Manual paper-based indenting and sanction delays across district offices.
4. Unverifiable proof-of-delivery reliant on vulnerable physical peon books.

Intended outcomes:
Deliver a unified, GIGW-compliant digital platform that guarantees 100% real-time stock visibility, automates 3-step Goods Receipt Notes (GRN), enforces CAG 3.5% spoilage norms, and provides closed-loop cryptographic QR Proof-of-Delivery across Assam.

2. PROPOSED SOLUTION & KEY FEATURES (Word Count: ~440 words)
======================================================================

MudraKosh ("Press & Stationery Treasury" / মুদ্ৰাকোষ) is a purpose-built Gov-Tech digital inventory and movement-tracking platform designed specifically for the Directorate of Printing & Stationery, Assam. It replaces fragmented paper registers with a unified, tamper-evident append-only ledger and mobile field verification suite.

Core Modules & Capabilities:
1. Secure Authentication & RBAC Gateway (/login): Features a bilingual interface (English / অসমীয়া), 6 distinct persona dashboards, multi-factor login (Password, NIC SMS OTP, Parichay SSO), and 1-tap role switching for rapid administrative oversight.
2. Executive Central Dashboard (/dashboard): Delivers real-time operational visibility over active inventory tonnage (MT, reels, reams), live dispatches, automated low-stock alert banners, and machine spoilage compliance.
3. Perpetual Stock Ledger (/inventory/stock): An immutable, transaction-derived ledger with row-level locking (zero overselling) and a slide-out audit trail drawer logging timestamps, user IDs, and IP addresses.
4. 3-Step Inward GRN Wizard (/inventory/inward): Captures vendor PO data, gross/tare digital weighbridge slips, and quality checks (GSM, moisture, burst factor). Generates unique GS1 QR reel tags for physical slotting into designated warehouse bays and racks.
5. Press Floor Spoilage Accounting Engine (/press/jobs): Tracks raw paper reel issuance to specific gazette/form print jobs, records finished copies, and algorithmically calculates machine trimming spoilage against the statutory 3.5% CAG ceiling, with automated Return-to-Store (RTS) credit logging.
6. Department Indents & Quota Management (/indents): Online requisition portal for state DDOs, checking requests against authorized annual departmental quotas and enabling 1-click digital sanctions by the Directorate.
7. Outward Dispatch & Cryptographic e-Challan (/dispatches): Generates tamper-evident dispatch manifests and HMAC-SHA256 QR-coded e-Challans.
8. Mobile Field Verification Suite: Lightweight, offline-capable PWA/mobile module for warehouse QR scanning, security gate pass issuance, driver delivery manifests, and glass-signature/OTP Proof-of-Delivery (PoD).
9. Statutory CAG Form 18 Reports (/reports): 1-click generation of audit-compliant Monthly Stock Accounts, Departmental Summaries, and Spoilage Variance statements.

User Interaction & What Changes Versus Today:
- Instead of manual ledger balancing, stock balances update deterministically upon authorized transaction sign-offs.
- Instead of unmonitored press trimming, spoilage is mathematically bounded and accounted for.
- Instead of physical peon books, deliveries are cryptographically confirmed on glass by recipient DDOs via smartphone camera, instantly syncing delivery confirmation back to the central ledger.

Current State of Development:
A complete, functioning prototype has been built and tested with synthetic data reflecting Guwahati Central Depot operations, featuring 9 responsive web views and 4 mobile field screens.

4. TECHNOLOGY & SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE (Word Count: ~450 words)
======================================================================

MudraKosh is designed around an open-standards, cloud-ready, and offline-first architecture complying with Government of India and Assam State Data Centre guidelines.

Five-Layer System Architecture:
1. Presentation & User Experience Layer:
   - Web Portal: React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vite. High-contrast UI adhering strictly to Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0), bilingual in English and Assamese (অসমীয়া).
   - Mobile Field Suite: Progressive Web App (PWA) and React Native application with HTML5 Camera QR scanner and touch-glass signature canvas.
2. Ingestion & API Gateway Layer:
   - NestJS (Node.js/TypeScript) REST API with strict DTO schema validation, rate-limiting, and CORS policies.
   - Authentication via JWT, RBAC middleware, and integration readiness for e-Pramaan / Jan Parichay National Single Sign-On.
   - Data Ingestion Engine supporting Excel/CSV bulk opening balance imports with pre-commit schema validation.
3. Append-Only Transactional Ledger Layer:
   - Database: PostgreSQL 16 relational database with strict ACID transactional guarantees.
   - Concurrency Control: Implements row-level pessimistic locking (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) to guarantee zero overselling during concurrent department indent sanctions.
   - Ledger Immutability: System prohibits raw SQL `UPDATE` or `DELETE` operations on stock balances; balances are dynamically computed from an append-only audit trail of signed transactions (GRN, Work-Order, RTS, Challan).
4. Rules, Algorithms & Analytics Engine:
   - Deterministic Unit Conversion Engine: Handles fractional conversions across paper measurements: Metric Tonnes $ to $ Reels $ to $ Reams $ to $ Sheets, accounting for paper basis weight (GSM) and sheet area formulas.
   - Dynamic Reorder Level (ROL) Engine: Evaluates running lead time and daily consumption variance: $ROL = (\text{Avg Daily Consumption} \times \text{Lead Time}) + \text{Safety Stock}$.
   - Machine Spoilage Algorithmic Norms: Bounded by the 3.5% CAG ceiling: $\text{Spoilage \%} = (\text{Trimming Wastage Kg} / \text{Paper Issued Kg}) \times 100$.
   - Cryptographic Engine: HMAC-SHA256 signature generation for QR e-Challans, ensuring physical challans cannot be forged or tampered with in transit.
5. Storage & Hosting Layer:
   - MinIO / S3-compatible private object storage for archiving generated e-Challans, weighbridge slips, and signed PoD receipts.
   - Containerized deployment using Docker and Docker Compose, ready for immediate hosting on the Assam State Data Centre (SDC) or MeitY-empanelled cloud infrastructure (NIC / AWS GovCloud India).
   - Security: End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, and automated daily WAL archiving.

Offline Synchronization:
The mobile PWA utilizes local IndexedDB/SQLite storage to record gate passes, driver manifests, and delivery signatures in remote areas with zero network connectivity. Transactions queue locally and synchronize deterministically via WebSocket/HTTPS once cellular connectivity (4G/5G) is re-established.

11. INNOVATION & DIFFERENTIATION (Word Count: ~430 words)
======================================================================

MudraKosh does not claim abstract technological novelty; its innovation lies in deep contextual engineering: purpose-fitting modern digital ledger, QR tracking, and mobile sign-off technologies to the real operational constraints of a government press and stationery depot.

Key Differentiators Versus Existing Approaches & Generic ERPs:

1. Deterministic Multi-Tier Paper Unit Conversion Engine:
Generic inventory ERPs treat items as simple discrete pieces or uniform weights. In a government printing press, paper enters as gross metric tonnes at a weighbridge, is stored as heavy 500 Kg reels, is mounted on printing presses as continuous webs, and is issued to government offices as reams of 500 sheets or stitched pads. MudraKosh algorithmically maintains fractional precision across the entire conversion chain: $\text{MT}  to  \text{Reels}  to  \text{Reams}  to  \text{Sheets}$, eliminating discrepancies that have plagued manual registers.

2. Press Floor Spoilage Accounting & CAG Compliance:
Standard warehouse tools treat manufacturing as an external black hole. MudraKosh links the storehouse directly to the press floor: tracking raw paper reel issuance against specific publication work orders (Assam Gazettes, electoral forms), measuring printed output, calculating machine trimming spoilage against the statutory 3.5% CAG ceiling, and automatically creating Return-to-Store (RTS) credit entries for unconsumed paper.

3. Cryptographic Closed-Loop Delivery (Eliminating Peon Books):
Instead of paper challans that get lost or signed illegibly in physical peon books, MudraKosh generates cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 QR e-Challans. The recipient department DDO inspects the delivery and signs directly on glass via smartphone camera PWA or validates receipt via mobile OTP. This action cryptographically closes the dispatch record and reconciles state inventory in real time.

4. Offline-First Mobile Field Suite on Commodity Devices:
Rather than mandating expensive, proprietary rugged handheld barcode terminals that cost lakhs and require specialized maintenance, MudraKosh runs as a Progressive Web App on standard Android smartphones used by existing drivers and security guards, operating seamlessly even during cellular blackouts with local SQLite caching.
